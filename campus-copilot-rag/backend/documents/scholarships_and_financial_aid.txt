SECTION 3: SCHOLARSHIPS & TUITION FEE CONCESSIONS
3.1 Academic Merit Scholarship:
The university grants tuition fee waivers to the top 3 rank holders in each engineering department at the end of every academic year:
- Rank 1: 50% tuition fee waiver for the following academic year.
- Rank 2: 30% tuition fee waiver for the following academic year.
- Rank 3: 20% tuition fee waiver for the following academic year.
Eligibility condition: The candidate must have cleared all enrolled subjects in the first attempt with a minimum CGPA of 8.5.

3.2 First Generation Graduate Scholarship:
Students admitted through government counseling as First Generation Graduates (neither parent possesses an undergraduate degree) receive a state tuition grant of Rs. 25,000 per academic year, credited directly against college fees upon verification of the tahsildar certificate.

3.3 Sports Quota & National Representation:
Students representing the university at Inter-University, State, or National sports tournaments are eligible for a 100% hostel fee waiver and special examination scheduling assistance.
