SECTION 4: CAMPUS PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY & INTERNSHIP GUIDELINES
4.1 Placement Drive Registration Criteria:
To register with the University Training & Placement Cell (TPC) in the 7th semester, students must satisfy:
- Minimum cumulative CGPA of 7.00 with zero standing/active arrears (backlogs).
- Minimum 80% attendance in soft skills, aptitude training, and technical mock coding tests conducted by the placement division.

4.2 "Dream" Company & Multiple Offer Policy:
- A student securing an initial job offer with an Annual Cost to Company (CTC) below 6.5 LPA remains eligible to participate in 'Dream Company' recruitment drives offering 10.0 LPA or above.
- Once a student secures a Dream Offer (>= 10 LPA), their placement process is concluded, and they may not register for additional drives.

4.3 Final Semester 6-Month Full-Time Internship:
Students in the 8th semester who secure full-time internships with stipends of at least Rs. 20,000/month may convert their classroom project into an Industry Capstone Project upon signing a tripartite Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) between the college, employer, and student.
