SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE REGULATIONS & DETENTION POLICY
1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement:
Every registered undergraduate and postgraduate student must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each enrolled theory and laboratory course to be eligible to appear for the Semester End Examinations (SEE).

1.2 Medical Leave and Official Duty Condonation:
Students with attendance between 65% and 74.9% due to verified medical illness or representing the college in authorized sports/hackathons may apply for Condonation of Attendance.
- Medical certificates must be submitted to the Head of Department (HOD) within 3 working days of returning to campus.
- A condonation fee of Rs. 1,500 per subject must be paid through the student ERP portal upon approval from the Dean of Academic Affairs.

1.3 Academic Detention:
Any student securing less than 65% aggregate attendance in a semester will be strictly DETAINED. Detained students will not be permitted to sit for examinations and must re-register and repeat the semester coursework in the subsequent academic year.
