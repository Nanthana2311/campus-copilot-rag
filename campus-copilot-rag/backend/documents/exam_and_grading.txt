SECTION 2: EXAMINATION BYLAWS, GRADING SYSTEM & REVALUATION
2.1 10-Point Letter Grading Scale:
Student academic performance is evaluated using Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) on a 10-point scale:
- Grade 'O' (Outstanding): 90-100 marks | Grade Points: 10
- Grade 'A+' (Excellent): 80-89 marks | Grade Points: 9
- Grade 'A' (Very Good): 70-79 marks | Grade Points: 8
- Grade 'B+' (Good): 60-69 marks | Grade Points: 7
- Grade 'B' (Above Average): 50-59 marks | Grade Points: 6
- Grade 'C' (Pass): 45-49 marks | Grade Points: 5
- Grade 'RA' (Re-Appearance / Arrear): < 45 marks | Grade Points: 0

2.2 Passing Criteria:
A student must score a minimum of 45% in the Semester End Examination (theory) and an overall combined total of 50% (Continuous Internal Assessment 40% + Semester End Exam 60%) to earn course credits.

2.3 Revaluation & Answer Script Photocopy:
- Students may apply for a photocopy of evaluated answer scripts within 5 calendar days of result publication by paying Rs. 400 per paper.
- Revaluation applications must be submitted within 7 days of receiving the photocopy, accompanied by a fee of Rs. 800 per course.
- If revaluation results in a grade jump of 2 or more marks, the revised score will be updated on the permanent transcript.
